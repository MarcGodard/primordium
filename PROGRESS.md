- [x] **M10 Engulfing (first ecology).** A sticky cell (expressed adhesion past a floor) can grab a smaller cell on its patch (under 60% of its membrane) and take in all of it: residues come apart into its loose monomers, matter exact. ## Next

- **Bigger periodic table for scales 1+.** The explorer says `[2, 6, 6]` keeps a trivalent backbone and a binary heritable alphabet (Fl/Sa), so it is reachable: generalise the genetic code (codon width from the letter count), the heritable-pair roles (now Li/Fl by index) and per-letter constants, then retrain every table.

- **Reef surrogate.** Scale 5 has detail only. The twin ceiling is measured (98.7%, `reef-shadow`). The detail runs ~15k cells at ~1.4 ms a step, so a surrogate only pays for reefs many times larger; the fitting design is tile-level aggregation (far tiles as counts of bodies per lineage and size class with learned rates, lifted to detail when viewed), not per-body rates, which would lose each genome's traits.
- **Reef body size vs evolution.** With the 2-cell grazing refuge, genomes and generations climb but the biggest bodies are ~100 cells instead of ~240, and most bodies are single cells living among the multicellular ones. Watch whether long runs grow bodies back as height/skin regulation spreads.
- **Reef body plans.** Bodies differentiate by light (within-body spread of glue and pigment 0.4-0.7 of the reef's, at noon) but no height- or skin-switched gene survives: the 104 founder genes on those signals are gone by day 100. Generations are slow (cells average ~2 spore generations in 600 days, max ~14) because coast cells divide every ~10 days; a somatic-damage aging rule (cells die faster per inherited copy error) was tried and dropped: mean load stayed ~0.3 errors, no effect on turnover. Long runs (6000 days, ~7 min) are the lever.
- **Reef cells outside the coast's experience.** Deep, dark or canopy-shaded cells fall in thin bins of the frozen lineage table (logged as discoveries). Options: re-train the lineage model with a coast that includes such conditions, or let thin-bin cells lift into a scale-3 detail world.

- **Heredity below the error threshold (Eigen's paradox): reached on the coast in the long run** (see log, 2000 days); a single crowded beach still sits above it. With pocket-catalysed proofreading, bit error is ~4.5% per division (130-bit genomes need < ~0.8%) and polymerase strength creeps up 0.24 -> 0.27 over 400 days, then stalls: genomes cannot hold improvements at this error. Free proofreading for every cell reaches 0.57% but removes the pressure on polymerases. Levers left: per-letter error is driven by scarce Li (wrong letters dominate the pool when copying starts), so supply-gated forks or Li-selective metabolism; shorter genomes; repair genes.
- **Niches by elevation:** patches differ in wet/dry and UV but phenotypes do not yet sort by them.
- **Coast lineage surrogate: long-run drift.** 98.2% of ceiling over 40 days after a 120-day warm-up, but 96.2% after a 200-day one, and the polymerase mean runs ~1 point under detail. Candidates: fitness interactions with climate (the factored model cannot express them), the 30-day memory.
- **Coast lineage at the poles with engulfing.** Five-window mean 95.3%; per-site divisions miss at both poles in opposite directions (see log). Ruled out (five-window mean unchanged within noise): a 10-day mean kT axis (95.1%), meals feeding lineage growth (95.2%, kept as more faithful), colony membership as a fitness axis (95.2%).
- **Coast lineage ceiling.** At 96.4% of a ~97% twin ceiling (five-window mean), the remaining gap has resisted: seasonal kT lag, meal-fed growth, colony membership as a feature, site calibration, longer or split table memory and periodic re-anchoring (one detailed day every 5 or 10: 96.0/96.2%, slower) all failed to beat it. Likely the honest limit of a per-cell hazard model: it cannot carry the hidden physiology (expression history, Li stores) that decides when a detailed cell divides.
- **Coast lineage: window-to-window spread.** Averaged over five warm-ups (120-280 days) the coast shadow is 97.1% of ceiling, single windows 94.9-98.3%. Tried and rejected: a longer memory for the conditions table (to remember seasons): 95.8% mean; 120 days for all tables: 96.3%.
- **Cell copy-mode churn (cosmetic).** Single-beach cells flip between detail and surrogate copying thousands of times (lifts ~2000 by day 60) as the day's temperature cycle moves every cell into thin kT bins together. Harmless to accuracy (both modes run the same interior). A 5-day minimum stay made all cells sit in detail for days at a time and cost accuracy (100.1 -> 98.5%); a wider kT axis did not help.
- **One shared cell-copy table across coast sites** (each world trains its own; discoveries fill with untrained-state notices).
- **Scale 1 cost:** folding dominates every scale above; the blend predicts well near cached relatives but cannot reach novel sequences, so scales 2/3 are no faster in surrogate mode. A lineage-level surrogate (a cell genome -> division time and mutation spectrum) is what would buy speed for the scales above the cell.
