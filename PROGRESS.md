# Progress

## Milestones

- [x] **M1 Scale 0 chemistry + environment + viewer.** Shell-rule elements, cosmos drivers, lattice chemistry with exact matter/energy ledger, Godot beach view with atom-anatomy zoom, orrery, census, inspect.
- [x] **M2 Surrogate framework + scale-0 surrogate.** Beach cut into 8 elevation bands; each runs lattice or mass-action counts (`hybrid.rs`, `kinetics.rs`). Rates learned per environment bin (`surrogate.rs` RateTable) from a year of detailed tallies (`primordium train` -> `data/rates0.json`). Confidence from bin coverage; low confidence lifts the band back to atoms and logs a discovery. Viewer: surrogate bands show their bond-type histogram; zooming in lifts visible bands to real atoms. Ledger exact through restrict/step/lift.
- [x] **M3 Scale 1 polymers.** Ka-backbone chains with 5 side-group letters (`polymer.rs`): lattice folding scored with scale-0 energies, function read off the fold ensemble (pocket catalysis per scale-0 bond pair, temperature-dependent stability, amphiphile, pigment). Tide pool (`pool.rs`) runs on learned scale-0 Ka-Ka rates: templated copying in the dry with physics-derived fidelity, melting in the wet, hydrolysis, ligation. Function surrogate = cache + Hamming nearest neighbour with confidence. Viewer scale 1 (Tab): pool of folds, close-up with pockets, click residue for its atoms.
- [x] **M4 Scale 2 protocells.** `protocell.rs`: amphiphile chains nucleate vesicles around pond water; membranes grow by insertion and osmotic-weighted adsorption (Chen-Roberts-Szostak), turn over, erode under UV (pigment shades), divide past a size limit under tidal shear, burst when small. Interiors run scale-1 chemistry plus metabolism (Ka + side atom -> monomer at learned scale-0 rates, catalysable). Tides feed the pond from an ocean reservoir and wash it out (matter ledger includes the ocean). Vesicle surrogate: learned copy/fidelity/synthesis/decay/ligation/break rates over the vesicle's catalytic profile, genome kept. Folding is batched and parallel (rayon). Viewer scale 2: pond -> vesicle interior -> chain fold -> residue atoms.
- [x] **M11 Mates signal.** Promoters grow to 5 bits (3 signal bits): a gene can also follow how many colony mates its cell has (0 alone, 1 at the colony cap), kept on each cell by the colony update. Codes 5-7 are unassigned and read as constitutive. The hook for jobs that depend on colony life (hunting alone, sharing inside).
- [x] **M10 Engulfing (first ecology).** A sticky cell (expressed adhesion past a floor) can grab a smaller cell on its patch (under 60% of its membrane) and take in all of it: residues come apart into its loose monomers, matter exact. Attempts scale with adhesion and patch crowding; colony mates hold on to each other (a member resists with odds 1 - e^(-0.5 x mates)), and no cell eats its own colony. Runs identically in detail and lineage mode. Lineage cells now grow and split in half like detailed cells (they were full-size clones, which broke the size rule engulfing depends on).
- [x] **M9 Gene regulation.** Each gene's promoter is read from the 4 bits before its leading stop codon (overlapping the previous gene's tail): signal (none, light, cold, Li scarcity), sign, gain. Transcription scales with a sigmoid of the signal, so a product can be made only when it pays. Promoters mutate like any bits. Viewer: gene list shows each promoter (`light+` = on in light).
- [x] **M8 Colonies (first multicellularity).** Folded products now read out adhesion (oily residues left exposed on a stable fold; exposing them costs stability). A dividing cell keeps its daughter stuck at odds that rise with the adhesion it expresses. Colony members anchor (no drifting between patches, no riding currents to other sites), share loose monomers toward the colony mean (exact whole-monomer transfers) and sit under the colony's mean pigment shade; past 16 members a random cell breaks away. Lineage mode inherits colonies the same way. Viewer: gold bridges join colony members (scales 3 and 4), colony share and adhesion per site.
- [x] **M6 Scale 4 coast (detail).** `coast.rs`: a coastline of scale-3 worlds under one sky at latitudes -63..+63 degrees, tidal range funnelled x1.8 in the end bays; flooded cells ride along-shore currents to neighbouring sites, carrying their matter (closed across sites). Sites step in parallel. `primordium coast`. Viewer scale 4: north-to-south strips with per-site climate, population, trait bars, an evolution map (which trait each site leads in over time); click a site and zoom for its real cells. Lineage surrogate (`lineage.rs`): a site's cells become lineages with frozen contents that divide and burst at learned rates, daughters paid for in matter from the patch.
- [x] **M5 Scale 3 cells.** `cell.rs`: binary genome (Li 0, Fl 1) in the same chemistry, 3-bit genetic code to the 5-letter working alphabet, genes = reading-frame runs between stop codons. Expression: messenger copy -> translation -> product chain folded by scale-1 physics; products are polymerases (copy speed + fidelity), metabolic catalysts, membrane builders, pigments. Division gated on a full genome copy; UV and cosmic rays mutate genomes; beach patches by elevation are niches fed and washed by tides. Cell surrogate: learned growth/replication/fidelity over phenotype + patch environment. Viewer scale 3: beach of cells -> genome ring with genes by role -> product fold -> residue atoms, plus population-mean chart.

## Next

- **Heredity below the error threshold (Eigen's paradox): reached on the coast in the long run** (see log, 2000 days); a single crowded beach still sits above it. With pocket-catalysed proofreading, bit error is ~4.5% per division (130-bit genomes need < ~0.8%) and polymerase strength creeps up 0.24 -> 0.27 over 400 days, then stalls: genomes cannot hold improvements at this error. Free proofreading for every cell reaches 0.57% but removes the pressure on polymerases. Levers left: per-letter error is driven by scarce Li (wrong letters dominate the pool when copying starts), so supply-gated forks or Li-selective metabolism; shorter genomes; repair genes.
- **Niches by elevation:** patches differ in wet/dry and UV but phenotypes do not yet sort by them.
- **Coast lineage surrogate: long-run drift.** 98.2% of ceiling over 40 days after a 120-day warm-up, but 96.2% after a 200-day one, and the polymerase mean runs ~1 point under detail. Candidates: fitness interactions with climate (the factored model cannot express them), the 30-day memory.
- **Coast lineage at the poles with engulfing.** Five-window mean 95.3%; per-site divisions miss at both poles in opposite directions (see log). Ruled out (five-window mean unchanged within noise): a 10-day mean kT axis (95.1%), meals feeding lineage growth (95.2%, kept as more faithful), colony membership as a fitness axis (95.2%).
- **Coast lineage: window-to-window spread.** Averaged over five warm-ups (120-280 days) the coast shadow is 97.1% of ceiling, single windows 94.9-98.3%. Tried and rejected: a longer memory for the conditions table (to remember seasons): 95.8% mean; 120 days for all tables: 96.3%.
- **One shared cell-copy table across coast sites** (each world trains its own; discoveries fill with untrained-state notices).
- **Scale 1 cost:** folding dominates every scale above; the blend predicts well near cached relatives but cannot reach novel sequences, so scales 2/3 are no faster in surrogate mode. A lineage-level surrogate (a cell genome -> division time and mutation spectrum) is what would buy speed for the scales above the cell.

- 2026-10-23 Proofreading. A mismatched growing end frays (odds 1 - e^(-gain * pairing shortfall / kT), the rule the pool already uses for stalls); in cells the polymerase pocket clips it back into the pool and retries, at odds 1 - e^(-poly/kT) since clipping is the pocket's reverse reaction. Retries cost copying time. Effects: selection for polymerase is now the strongest gradient seen (r = +0.10..+0.14 vs lineage growth at days 80-140, was +0.06..+0.09), bit error 6-7% -> ~4.5% after 400 days. Cell surrogate learns clip odds per (template, partner); its table now needs 400 samples per bin before it is trusted (with 6, surrogate bit error ran at 9% on thin bins). Cell shadow 99.3-100.1% of ceiling.
