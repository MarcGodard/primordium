- [x] **M5 Scale 3 cells.** `cell.rs`: binary genome (Li 0, Fl 1) in the same chemistry, 3-bit genetic code to the 5-letter working alphabet, genes = reading-frame runs between stop codons. Expression: messenger copy -> translation -> product chain folded by scale-1 physics; products are polymerases (copy speed + fidelity), metabolic catalysts, membrane builders, pigments. Division gated on a full genome copy; UV and cosmic rays mutate genomes; beach patches by elevation are niches fed and washed by tides. Cell surrogate: learned growth/replication/fidelity over phenotype + patch environment. Viewer scale 3: beach of cells -> genome ring with genes by role -> product fold -> residue atoms, plus population-mean chart.

## Log

- 2026-10-23 M5: the chemistry forces a genetic code. Double copying replaces each letter with its best partner's best partner; only Li <-> Fl is an involution, so Hy/Ox/Bo information is erased in two generations. Heritable alphabet is binary; the working alphabet is reached by translation. Cell surrogate shadow (`primordium cell-shadow`): 93.2% of the detail-twin ceiling (first versions 57% and 90.8%: training counted shared membrane physics, then idle forks). Selection (`primordium cell-selection`): every gene trait correlates positively with lineage growth (gene count +0.10..+0.14, amphiphile +0.07..+0.10), unlike scale 2. Gradients are still weak against crowding noise; means drift slowly. Open: cell surrogate lift churn (cells flip modes often; harmless since products freeze), selection strength, spatial niches by elevation not yet pronounced.
- 2026-10-23 M4: vesicle surrogate shadow (`primordium pond-shadow`): 88.7% agreement on population stats vs 96.5% detail-twin ceiling = **91.9% of ceiling** (first version missed ligation/decay channels: 17.7%). No speed gain at scale 2 yet: scale-1 folding dominates either way. Selection (`primordium selection`): osmotic load predicts lineage growth (r up to +0.29) but interior catalysis does not; catalysis-coupled fidelity drops pond copy error to ~5%, yet vesicles rarely hold a catalyst long enough (error threshold inside ~25%). Compartments alone do not select catalysts here because division is not gated on copying anything: that is M5's genome-gated division.

Copy error ~25-29% per letter: heredity only for short motifs until catalysts improve fidelity. Backbone catalysis is pool-wide, so no selection for it yet: that needs compartments (M4).
